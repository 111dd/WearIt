Dor requested fast photos on every product, explainable personal fit percentages, automatic wardrobe purchase import with date/store/optional receipt, weather-aware variety without blacklisting the grey G-Star shorts, working offers, and efficient rendering. ## Implemented locally

- Photos: three shared preview jobs, stable URL/preference identity (not catalog fetch time), first decoded shop image published before Vision, optional three-second refinement to an item-only angle, model-photo fallback, six bounded candidate URLs, 480px decoding, 48-entry memory cache, short failed-cache lifetime, one automatic retry and a manual retry action. Cancelled consumers release waiters; running jobs retain their concurrency slot until completion. The importer separately downloads up to 1600px and runs cutout work in an actor.
- Ranking: value snapshots into a serial `ShopRankingWorker`; no live SwiftData model/context crosses the boundary. Worker-local models are never inserted. Cancelled or stale results cannot replace a newer screen. Browsing uses cancellable background work and stable category/type/brand round-robin instead of catalog order. Price formatting avoids constructing a formatter per tile. Warmth calculations are reused across outfit permutations.
- Clothes first in For You and Explore. Accessories have an explicit category; an occasional optional accessory may accompany a complete clothing look. The deep pass is capped at 70 clothing + 10 accessory candidates. It reserves offer candidates before truncation and keeps qualified offers beyond the default feed cutoff.
- Fit is an **estimated index**, not measured accuracy or purchase probability. Personal cards expose taste (50%), evaluated wardrobe combinations (35%), and actual weather comfort (15%); absent inputs are omitted and weights renormalized. Browsing has only the cheap taste estimate and explicitly explains that combinations/weather were not evaluated. Discounts do not inflate fit. Early-history language is shown with fewer than ten wear-day/shopping signals. Future-season heuristics do not claim actual weather evidence.
- `I bought it` saves a real Garment before hiding/training. Identity/URL prevents rapid duplicate imports; exact existing retailer-source matches can reuse an item. In-stock usual size is marked inferred. Date is the confirmation timestamp, editable, explicitly labeled as such; no historic purchase date is guessed from `createdAt`. Store and public product URL are durable fields. Photo processing/save is asynchronous, deletion-safe, and preserves a manually chosen image. The ten-second notice exposes details/receipt and Undo. Undo removes only newly inserted garments, or restores automatic facts on an existing item, and reverses that purchase's feedback delta without discarding other interactions.
- Optional receipt photo/camera attachment is downsampled/encoded off-main, stripped of original image metadata, saved as optional external-storage Data with the garment, privately synced through the existing SwiftData/CloudKit store, and included in account export. No OCR/receipt extraction is claimed. Existing exact linked purchases can recover their recorded date/store from old feedback; no fuzzy matching and no bulk insertion of historic purchases. Manual corrections/unknown dates remain authoritative.
- Weather history deduplicates wear/comfort events per plan and day/evening. Explicit comfort feedback supersedes a wear signal; wearing alone is weaker, and general love is not thermal evidence. Nearest-temperature and rain evidence is shrunk toward physical defaults, preferring the actual item over type transfer. Exposed legs/feet receive a continuous cool-weather score, not an item/brand/month blacklist. This score is shared by the planner, replacements and shopping. Stored planned looks and locked items are retained. No actual on-device G-Star record was inspected.
- Preview diversity rotates all owned pieces, with extra weight on bottoms, within 0.06 of the strongest compatible option. Three bounded partner pools keep three useful favorites and rotate up to six other appropriate items per category across the feed; daily rotation reaches more of the wardrobe without enumerating it all. Saved upcoming assignments are not rewritten.
